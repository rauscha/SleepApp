The train rocks just slightly. Not enough to disturb you. Just enough to remind you that you are moving, that you are being carried, that there is nothing required of you right now except to let yourself be carried. The rails beneath you produce a sound that is less a sound and more a rhythm, a low, steady conversation between metal and motion. It goes on and on, the same and the same, a little slower than you expect, a little quieter than you remember. There is heat in the cabin.
