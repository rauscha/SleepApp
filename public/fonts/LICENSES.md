# Self-hosted font licenses

Both fonts here are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL),
which permits bundling and self-hosting (including in a commercial product)
provided the fonts aren't sold on their own and the OFL notice travels with
them. They are served from `/fonts/` so the app works fully offline once the
service worker has cached them — no third-party font-origin round-trip.

## InterVariable.woff2
- Family: Inter (variable, weights 100–900)
- Designer: Rasmus Andersson
- Source: https://github.com/rsms/inter (OFL-1.1)

## EBGaramond-latin.woff2
- Family: EB Garamond (variable latin subset, used at weights 400 & 500)
- Designer: Georg Duffner; Octavio Pardo (variable axes)
- Source: https://github.com/octaviopardo/EBGaramond12 — the exact latin
  subset Google Fonts serves (fonts.gstatic.com EB Garamond v32). OFL-1.1.
